# Fan needs on price, fees, fairness and trust (US focus, NYC/NYS; UK/EU where strong), 2023–2026

Method note: research done 2026-09-28 via web search. Direct page fetches to governor.ny.gov, nysenate.gov, nclnet.org, ticketnews.com and sportsfans.org were blocked by the sandbox proxy, so many findings rest on search-result snippets (cited with the URL they came from). Where a snippet was the only evidence, treat the exact wording as "as reported".

## Ranked fan needs (money-and-fairness side), with the strongest supporting evidence

### Takeaway
In order of evidence strength and intensity: (1) see the real all-in price up front, with no checkout surprises; (2) lower or fewer fees and a sense of "affordable", since fees drive most of the anger; (3) fair access that doesn't lose out to bots, brokers and speculative listings; (4) no surge or dynamic pricing, or at least transparency about it; (5) the ability to resell at a fair price, with most fans wanting a cap near face value rather than a ban on transfer; (6) protection from fake or scam tickets; (7) clear refunds when an event is cancelled or rescheduled.

### Cited Findings
**1. All-in price transparency / hidden fees**
- Consumer Reports: 44% of Americans reported unexpected fees added to concert and sports tickets. A later CR analysis found 45% of people who had used live-entertainment or sports services in the past two years hit unexpected fees. 85% had met a hidden fee on some service, and 96% found hidden fees annoying. — [Consumer Reports, Protect Yourself From Hidden Fees](https://www.consumerreports.org/money/fees-billing/protect-yourself-from-hidden-fees-a1096754265/); [CR advocacy: urges FTC to finalize junk fee ban](https://advocacy.consumerreports.org/press_release/consumer-reports-urges-ftc-to-finalize-proposed-ban-on-costly-junk-fees); [Inquirer on the CR survey (2019 baseline)](https://www.inquirer.com/business/hidden-fees-cable-bills-consumer-reports-survey-20190529.html)
- A search snippet from a PRWeb release says "nearly half of consumers say they're attending fewer live events than they did three years ago, citing ticket prices and hidden fees as the biggest deterrents." The release is commissioned and vendor-originated, so this is lower-quality evidence. — [PRWeb: Live Events Now a Luxury](https://www.prweb.com/releases/live-events-now-a-luxury-new-data-shows-ticket-prices-driving-out-average-americans-amid-trump-crackdown-on-scalpers-302420550.html)
- The FTC alleges (Sept 2025 complaint) that Ticketmaster's mandatory fees raise checkout prices by 24%–44%, totalling $6.4B from 2019 to 2024. — [FTC press release, Sept 2025](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-sues-live-nation-ticketmaster-engaging-illegal-ticket-resale-tactics-deceiving-artists-consumers); [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/09/18/ftc-sues-live-nation-ticketmaster-for-illegal-ticket-resale-tactics-bots-act-violations/)
- There are signs that fees were shifted rather than cut after all-in pricing: TicketNews (Mar 2026) reports internal Ticketmaster documents showing fees "quietly shifted" under all-in pricing. — [TicketNews, Mar 2026](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/ticketmaster-championed-all-in-pricing-internal-documents-show-how-fees-were-quietly-shifted/)

**2. Price level / affordability**
- YouGov: 76% of consumers call concert tickets unaffordable (as reported by an aggregator snippet). — [YouGov, dynamic pricing 2024](https://yougov.com/articles/49709-fair-unfair-consumer-opinion-on-dynamic-pricing-2024) (via search snippet; the 76% figure may come from a different YouGov item)
- CouponCabin survey: more than 90% believe concert tickets are overpriced. Fans still attended an average of 7 shows in 2024, which fits the "funflation" pattern: people complain and keep buying. — [CouponCabin](https://www.couponcabin.com/blog/infographic-the-cost-of-live-music/); [CNBC, Jan 2025](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/01/31/funflation-concert-ticket-prices-soar-but-music-fans-dont-care.html)

**3. Fair access vs bots, brokers and speculative listings**
- The Breakwater Strategy poll for the Music Artists Coalition (released May 2026, publicised by NCL) found: 82% support a ban on speculative ticket sales; 84% say brokers should be legally barred from selling tickets they don't own; 84% say the industry needs common-sense reforms; only 42% think the market operates fairly. — [NCL, May 2026](https://nclnet.org/new-poll-massive-public-support-for-ticketing-reforms-as-the-national-consumers-league-demands-full-senate-vote-on-the-ticket-act/); [TheTicketingBusiness, May 2026](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/05/national-consumer-league-calls-for-senate-vote-on-ticket-act/)
- Counterpoint: the Sports Fans Coalition, which is pro-resale, argues the same poll shows Americans value resale as consumer choice and rank resale price caps among their *least-preferred* reforms. I could not fetch the page to verify the numbers. — [Sports Fans Coalition](https://www.sportsfans.org/latest-news/music-artists-coalitions-poll-makes-the-case-for-a-well-regulated-ticket-resale-market)
- The same poll, as reported, found 78% support an industry-wide cap on resale markups and 85% support mandatory face-value disclosure. These numbers come via a snippet and are attributed to "voters nationally". — [Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/survey-shows-fans-and-artists-demand-ticket-resale-reform/)
- Eras Tour presale (Nov 2022): 3.5B system requests (4x the previous peak). 1.5M fans had codes but about 14M users, including bots, arrived at once. Data for Progress found 40% of voters viewed Ticketmaster unfavourably. — [Ticketmaster Business](https://business.ticketmaster.com/press-release/taylor-swift-the-eras-tour-onsale-explained/); [Data for Progress](https://www.dataforprogress.org/blog/2022/11/30/ticketmaster-and-live-nation-you-need-to-calm-down); [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift%E2%80%93Ticketmaster_controversy)

**4. Dynamic pricing backlash**
- YouGov US: 49% find dynamic pricing for live concerts unfair vs about a third who find it fair. 75% say it makes it hard for fans to see their favourite artists. Younger fans are more accepting if the extra money goes to the artist (68% of 18–34s would pay extra) or if it cuts out resellers (64%). — [YouGov US](https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/46438-consumer-view-dynamic-pricing-more-acceptable-if-money-goes-to-artist-and-cuts-out-re-sellers); [YouGov 2024](https://yougov.com/articles/49709-fair-unfair-consumer-opinion-on-dynamic-pricing-2024)
- YouGov: 58% in GB and 58% in Canada find dynamic pricing unfair for sporting events. 51–57% of US consumers find it unfair for venues such as theme parks. — [YouGov 2024](https://yougov.com/articles/49709-fair-unfair-consumer-opinion-on-dynamic-pricing-2024)
- UK: 71% of Britons oppose dynamic ticket pricing (Jan 2023 poll). — [TicketNews, Jan 2023](https://www.ticketnews.com/2023/01/poll-71-percent-of-britons-are-against-dynamic-ticket-pricing/)
- UK "Music Fans' Voice" survey (8,000+ respondents, Feb–Mar 2025): 91% think dynamic/surge pricing should be outlawed. 97% think face-value resale should be allowed, and 91% want legislation against resale at much higher prices. — [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/artists/shows-festivals/91-percent-believe-dynamic-pricing-should-be-outlawed-music-fans-have-spoken-the-music-fans-voice-survey-has-just-released-its-results); [TheTicketingBusiness, Apr 2025](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2025/04/live-music-fans-against-the-use-of-dynamic-pricing-reveals-report/)

**5. Resale caps and transferability**
- As reported, 75% of surveyed fans want resale capped at 20% or less above face value, and 87% support authorised resale services. The snippet doesn't clearly say which survey this is (likely UK). — [Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/survey-shows-fans-and-artists-demand-ticket-resale-reform/)
- DICE model: resale and transfer are allowed only through its Wait List or a "Limited Right to Transfer". Resale never goes above face value, and a seller gets a full refund when a waitlisted fan buys the ticket. The common complaint is that you can't resell if the show isn't sold out, so money is lost on an unused ticket. — [DICE: Wait list explained](https://dicefm.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19958073128849-The-wait-list-explained); [DICE US Purchase Terms](https://dicefm.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4412965076881-United-States-Purchase-Terms); [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33669715)

**6. Trust: scams and fake tickets**
- Lloyds Bank (Apr 2025): Oasis fans made up 56% of reported concert-ticket scams that year, losing £436 on average. There were more than 1,000 cases among Lloyds customers, and an estimated 5,000+ UK victims lost over £2M in total. More than 90% of cases began on Meta platforms, and 35–44-year-olds made up 30% of cases. — [Insider Media](https://www.insidermedia.com/news/national/oasis-reunion-tour-2025-ticket-scams-cost-fans-more-than-2m-lloyds-bank-says); [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/04/24/lloyds-oasis-fans-getting-ticket-scammed/)
- Eras Tour UK scams: about £1M lost (Apr 2024). — [Telegraph via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/uk/the-daily-telegraph/20240417/281715504660603)
- The BBB reports hundreds of ticket-scam complaints. — [BBB tip](https://www.bbb.org/article/tips/13986-bbb-tip-buying-tickets)
- More fans are buying "ticket protection" as prices rise (Mar 2026). — [TicketNews, Mar 2026](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/report-rising-ticket-prices-drives-fans-ticket-protection/)

**7. Refunds**
- New York is reported to require a full refund within 30 days if a show is cancelled or its date changes, and California uses a 30-day refund timeline (B&P Code §22507). Ticketmaster and StubHub refund cancelled events but, for postponed ones, generally only at the organiser's discretion. Fans complain that "postponed" is used to hold money for months. — [Sauder Schelkopf](https://sauderschelkopf.com/blog/canceled-festivals-in-2025-your-legal-rights-how-to-get-a-refund/); [California Today](https://californiatoday.com/guides/tickets/ticket-refund-rights-2026/); [Ticketmaster blog](https://blog.ticketmaster.com/refund-credit-canceled-postponed-rescheduled-events/)

### Inferences
- The strongest, least disputed fan need is price transparency: it has the most survey support and has already been written into federal and NY law. Across polls, fees generate more anger than face value.
- Bans on speculative listings and "sell only what you own" rules poll at 80%+. Resale caps poll well in UK surveys and in the artist-funded US poll, but US pro-resale groups dispute that. Transferability is a real US value: NY guarantees it, and fans dislike being unable to sell unwanted tickets.
- Fans tolerate higher prices when the money visibly goes to the artist or cuts out scalpers (YouGov 18–34 data). Framing matters.
- For Exos, a small NYC platform, the opportunity is to match or beat the law. That means showing the all-in price on the first screen, keeping fees low and itemised, using a face-value fan-to-fan resale or waitlist that still lets fans recoup money even when a show isn't sold out (fixing DICE's gap), and stating a written refund policy that covers "postponed".

### Gaps
- I found no 2023–2026 Morning Consult ticketing poll with percentages. Eventbrite and DICE buyer surveys with fee or fairness percentages were also not found.
- I could not verify the exact source or date of the YouGov "76% unaffordable" figure or the Hypebot "75% want ≤20% cap" figure. Both come from snippets.
- Fan Fair Alliance's own 2023–2026 survey percentages were not found. The UK "Music Fans' Voice" survey is the closest.
- Reddit fan sentiment (r/concerts, r/Ticketmaster, r/StubHub, r/nyc) wasn't collected because of the tool budget and proxy limits.

## Current rules: FTC, New York, BOTS Act, TICKET Act, state transferability, UK CMA and resale caps

### Takeaway
All-in pricing is now required both federally (FTC rule, effective May 12, 2025) and in New York (since Aug 29, 2022). BOTS Act enforcement restarted in 2025. The federal TICKET Act passed the House in April 2025 but was still awaiting a Senate floor vote in mid-2026. New York's broader 2025–2026 reform attempts (speculative-ticket ban, 15% fee cap) failed, so its existing Article 25 framework was simply extended. The UK has committed to banning resale above face value.

### Cited Findings
**US federal**
- FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees (16 CFR 464): announced Dec 2024, published in the Federal Register Jan 10, 2025, **effective May 12, 2025**. Live-event ticketing and short-term lodging must show the total price, including all mandatory fees, prominently and before checkout, including in ads. The total must be more prominent than any lower price. Bait-and-switch pricing is banned. — [FTC, Dec 2024](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/12/federal-trade-commission-announces-bipartisan-rule-banning-junk-ticket-hotel-fees); [FTC, May 2025](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/05/ftc-rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-take-effect-may-12-2025); [FTC FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions); [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/10/2024-30293/trade-regulation-rule-on-unfair-or-deceptive-fees)
- The rule excludes government taxes and optional fees from the total. Ticketmaster said it would display fees more clearly when the rule took effect. — [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/a-biden-era-junk-fee-rule-is-now-in-effect-ticketmaster-says-it-will-display-fees-more-clearly)
- BOTS Act (2016): it had been enforced only once before 2025. In March 2025 President Trump signed an executive order calling for more enforcement. In Aug 2025 the FTC sued Key Investment Group (TotalTickets.com), alleging it bought about 380,000 tickets (Nov 2022–Dec 2023; roughly $57M spent, $64M resold) through thousands of accounts, including 273 Eras tickets bought through 49 accounts to get around a 6-ticket limit. In Apr 2026 a Maryland federal judge let the case proceed, rejecting the argument that the Act covers only automated bots. — [FTC, Aug 2025](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/08/ftc-takes-action-against-ticket-resellers-using-illegal-tactics-bypass-ticket-limit-protections); [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/08/18/business/ftc-taylor-swift-eras-tour); [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/taylor-swift-eras-tour-ticket-reseller-lawsuit-ftc-bots-act/); [TicketNews, Apr 2026](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/04/federal-judge-lets-ftcs-bots-act-case-against-key-investment-group-move-forward/)
- A separate broker settled with the FTC over BOTS Act violations tied to Tate McRae and Megan Moroney tickets. — [Law Commentary](https://www.lawcommentary.com/articles/ticket-broker-settles-with-ftc-over-bots-act-violations-tied-to-tate-mcrae-megan-moroney-tickets)
- On Sept 17/18, 2025, the FTC and 7 states (CO, FL, IL, NE, TN, UT, VA) sued Live Nation/Ticketmaster under FTC Act §5 and the BOTS Act. They allege drip pricing, letting brokers exceed ticket limits (thousands of tickets per event), and failing to stop circumvention. — [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/ftc-sues-live-nation-ticketmaster-engaging-illegal-ticket-resale-tactics-deceiving-artists-consumers); [Alston & Bird](https://www.alston.com/en/insights/publications/2025/09/ftc-state-lawsuit-live-nation-ticketmaster)
- TICKET Act (H.R. 1402 / S. 281, 119th Congress): passed the House 409–15 on Apr 29, 2025. It requires all-in pricing, bans speculative ticketing (critics say Vivid Seats' "seat saver" survives), bans fake "official" claims and look-alike domains, and requires refunds for cancelled or postponed events. Senate Commerce reported S. 281 (S. Rept. 119-17). In May 2026 NCL was still demanding a full Senate floor vote, and a House hearing followed in July 2026, so it was **not enacted as of the latest sources**. — [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/04/29/ticket-act-passes-house-now-headed-to-senate/); [GovTrack S.281](https://www.govtrack.us/congress/bills/119/s281); [Senate Rept 119-17](https://www.congress.gov/committee-report/119th-congress/senate-report/17/1); [Recording Academy](https://www.recordingacademy.com/advocacy/news/house-of-representatives-passes-ticket-act); [TicketNews, May 2026](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/05/national-consumers-league-presses-senate-for-ticket-act-vote-as-ticketmaster-scrutiny-mounts/); [TicketNews, Jul 2026](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/07/national-consumers-league-pushes-for-gold-standard-ticketing-reform-at-house-of-representatives-hearing/)

**New York State (Arts and Cultural Affairs Law Art. 25)**
- 2022 law (S9461, Skoufis): signed June 30, 2022 and **effective Aug 29, 2022**. The all-in price, including fees, must be disclosed before the ticket is selected. Delivery fees on electronic or print-at-home tickets are banned. Resellers must disclose the original price. Sale of free tickets is outlawed. Penalties for bots were expanded. — [Governor's release](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-signs-legislation-targeting-unfair-ticketing-practices-live-event-industry); [Skift Meetings](https://meetings.skift.com/2022/07/27/ticket-fee-transparency-law-in-new-york-could-have-impact-across-the-events-industry/); [FKKS](https://advertisinglaw.fkks.com/post/102hvkw/new-york-enacts-fee-transparency-law-for-live-event-ticketing); [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/concert-tickets-hidden-fees-banned-new-york/); [NY DOS all-in pricing reminder, June 2023](https://dos.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2023/06/ticket-reseller-reminder.pdf)
- Transferability: ACA §25.30 bars operators from charging a different price based on a ticket's form or transferability. Being able to transfer a ticket independently of the operator can't be treated as a "special service" carrying a service charge. S276 (2025) describes this as preserving fans' "right to ticket transferability" to gift, transfer or sell. — [NYSenate ACA §25.30](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ACA/25.30); [FindLaw §25.30](https://codes.findlaw.com/ny/arts-and-cultural-affairs-law/aca-sect-25-30/); [NY Senate S276](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S276)
- 2025 reform bill S276 (Skoufis/Krueger/May) proposed a speculative-listing ban, a 15% cap on service charges, a ban on minimum resale prices, anti-bot and holdback transparency rules, and season-ticket-holder rights. It **did not pass** (reported as "watered down"). A modified 2026 "Affordable Concerts Act" also failed. — [TicketNews, Jan 2025](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/01/new-york-ticketing-legislation-filed-bots-holdbacks-transparency/); [Playbill](https://playbill.com/article/new-legislation-in-new-york-aims-to-curb-unfair-ticketing-practices-in-live-event-industry-including-broadway); [NYTix](https://www.nytix.com/news/new-york-ticket-law); [TicketNews, Feb 2026](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/02/new-yorks-proposed-affordable-concerts-act-multiple-ticketmaster-wish-list-items-sprinkled-with-some-meaningful-consumer-wins/); [TicketNews, Jun 2026](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/new-york-extends-ticket-laws-after-broader-resale-crackdown-fails-again/)
- Extender: A11558/S10640 passed both houses on **June 4, 2026**. If signed, it keeps Article 25 in force **through June 30, 2027**; the prior sunset was June 30, 2026. I could not confirm whether it has been signed. — [TicketNews, Jun 2026](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/new-york-extends-ticket-laws-after-broader-resale-crackdown-fails-again/); [NY Senate S10640](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S10640)
- NY resellers must be licensed with the Department of State (§25.13). — [NY DOS](https://dos.ny.gov/licensing-ticket-resellers); [ACA §25.13](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ACA/25.13)

**Other US states**
- California AB 1720 (Haney) would cap resale at face value + 10% and require all-in advertised prices. It passed the Assembly 49–16 and cleared three Senate committees by Aug 2026, but the Department of Finance opposes it. **Pending.** — [TicketNews, Aug 2026](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/california-ticketing-bills-progress-but-lawmakers-show-increasing-concern-over-proposed-price-cap/); [CA leginfo](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billStatusClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB1720); [CalMatters](https://calmatters.org/digital-democracy/2026/04/ticketmaster-resale-market/)

**UK**
- CMA and Oasis: an investigation opened in Sept 2024. On 25 Sept 2025 the CMA secured undertakings from Ticketmaster covering clearer pre-sale and in-queue pricing information, accurate ticket labelling, and compliance reports for 2 years, to be implemented within 6 weeks. The CMA found **no evidence of algorithmic dynamic pricing**. The problem was that fans in queues weren't told standing tickets were being sold at two price tiers. — [Taylor Wessing](https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/insights-and-events/insights/2025/10/rd-roll-with-it-ticketmaster-gives-cma-undertakings-on-ticket-pricing-transparency); [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/ticketmaster-didnt-use-dynamic-pricing-for-oasis-concerts-will-change-how-it-sells-tickets-in-uk-watchdog-says/); [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/09/29/ticketmaster-makes-voluntary-changes-following-oasis-tickets-investigation-in-uk/)

A July 2026 report says the ban is moving forward, but I couldn't confirm the commencement date. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/18/uk-government-ticket-resale-ban.html); [Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/11/19/uk-makes-plans-to-ban-for-profit-ticket-resale-official-reactions/); [Business Companion](https://www.businesscompanion.info/en/news-and-updates/government-bans-ticket-touting-to-protect-fans-from-rip-off-prices); [Euronews, Jan 2025](https://www.euronews.com/culture/2025/01/10/uk-government-plan-30-price-cap-on-resold-tickets); [Slaughter and May](https://www.slaughterandmay.com/insights/new-insights/at-face-value-the-king-s-speech-and-the-future-of-ticket-reselling-in-uk-sport/); [Hot Minute, Jul 2026](https://hotminute.co.uk/2026/07/15/uk-to-cap-concert-ticket-resale-prices-and-platform-fees/)

### Inferences
- An NYC platform operating in 2026 must already: show the all-in price (fees included) before selection (NY) and in any ad or listing (FTC); charge no delivery fee for e-tickets; allow transfer without a transferability surcharge (NY §25.30); and refund cancelled or rescheduled shows promptly. Speculative-listing bans are not yet law in NY or federally, but they're the most likely next rule.
- NY Article 25 runs on sunsets. It needs renewing every year or two, so compliance should be tracked against the extension status.

## How fans feel about dynamic pricing, non-transferable tickets (DICE model), face-value resale caps and refund policies

### Takeaway
Fans are strongly against dynamic pricing: about 49% of Americans call it unfair, and 71–91% of UK fans oppose it. UK fans overwhelmingly back face-value resale. US opinion on hard resale caps is contested, while support for "sell only what you own" and face-value disclosure is high. Non-transferable, face-value-only resale (DICE) wins goodwill, but a Wait List that works only for sold-out shows frustrates fans who get stuck.

### Cited Findings
- Dynamic pricing: see the YouGov US figures (49% unfair; 75% say it hurts access), YouGov GB/Canada (58% unfair for sports), UK polls (71% against in 2023; 91% want it outlawed in 2025). — [YouGov US](https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/46438-consumer-view-dynamic-pricing-more-acceptable-if-money-goes-to-artist-and-cuts-out-re-sellers); [TicketNews 2023](https://www.ticketnews.com/2023/01/poll-71-percent-of-britons-are-against-dynamic-ticket-pricing/); [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/artists/shows-festivals/91-percent-believe-dynamic-pricing-should-be-outlawed-music-fans-have-spoken-the-music-fans-voice-survey-has-just-released-its-results)
- Even without a real algorithm, fans read opaque tiering as "dynamic pricing" (the CMA's Oasis finding), so disclosure alone reduces the anger. — [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/ticketmaster-didnt-use-dynamic-pricing-for-oasis-concerts-will-change-how-it-sells-tickets-in-uk-watchdog-says/)
- Live Nation's CEO has argued that concerts are "underpriced", while Rolling Stone questions that. — [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/live-nationceo-concerts-aunderpriced-are-they-1235432347/)
- Resale caps: in the UK, 97% say face-value resale should be allowed and 91% want a law against resale at much higher prices. In the US, the MAC/Breakwater poll reports 78% support for markup caps, but the Sports Fans Coalition says caps rank low. — [TheTicketingBusiness](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2025/04/live-music-fans-against-the-use-of-dynamic-pricing-reveals-report/); [Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/survey-shows-fans-and-artists-demand-ticket-resale-reform/); [Sports Fans Coalition](https://www.sportsfans.org/latest-news/music-artists-coalitions-poll-makes-the-case-for-a-well-regulated-ticket-resale-market)
- Transferability as a value: the NY framework and pro-resale groups (Protect Ticket Rights) frame free transfer as a consumer right. — [Protect Ticket Rights](https://www.protectticketrights.com/news/86/PRESS+RELEASE+NY+State+Continues+to+Protect+Ticketholders+Ability+to+Freely+Transfer+Tickets)
- DICE: face value only; resale only through the Wait List (sellers get a full refund when their ticket resells) or a limited transfer; selling above the purchase price can get the ticket cancelled. Criticism: no exit if the show doesn't sell out. — [DICE](https://dicefm.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19958073128849-The-wait-list-explained); [DICE resale & scams](https://dicefm.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/18201556722961-Reselling-Tickets-on-DICE-and-Preventing-Scams); [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=33669715)
- Refund anger centres on the postponement loophole and slow refunds. Ticketmaster refunds cancelled events within 30 days, but postponed ones are at organiser discretion. — [Ticketmaster](https://blog.ticketmaster.com/refund-credit-canceled-postponed-rescheduled-events/); [TrustDALE](https://trustdale.com/blog/fans-vs-ticketmaster-inside-the-refund-fight)

### Inferences
- The best-supported middle ground is: transfers allowed, resale at face value (or capped) through an official channel, the face value always shown, and no algorithmic surge pricing. If tiers exist, name them in the queue.

### Gaps
- There's no good US survey measuring feelings about non-transferable tickets specifically. The evidence on DICE is anecdotal and qualitative.
- No survey numbers on refund-policy preferences were found.

## Trust issues with resale: fake tickets, speculative listings, marketplace guarantees

### Takeaway
The main trust problems are social-media scams (most UK scam cases start on Meta platforms), speculative "tickets" that the seller doesn't own (opposed by 82–84% in polling), look-alike "official" sites, and brokers getting around ticket limits, a practice the FTC alleges the primary seller tolerated. Marketplace guarantees (e.g., StubHub FanProtect) exist, but critics say "seat saver" style products are a way round speculative-ticket bans.

### Cited Findings
- UK scam data (Lloyds): Oasis fans lost more than £2M, with over 90% of cases starting on Meta platforms; Eras Tour scams cost about £1M. — [Insider Media](https://www.insidermedia.com/news/national/oasis-reunion-tour-2025-ticket-scams-cost-fans-more-than-2m-lloyds-bank-says); [Lloyds 2023 release](https://www.lloydsbankinggroup.com/media/press-releases/2023/lloyds-bank-2023/sharp-increase-concert-ticket-scams.html)
- Speculative listings: 82% support a ban and 84% say brokers shouldn't sell tickets they don't own. The TICKET Act as passed by the House is criticised for leaving Vivid Seats' "seat saver" legal. — [NCL](https://nclnet.org/new-poll-massive-public-support-for-ticketing-reforms-as-the-national-consumers-league-demands-full-senate-vote-on-the-ticket-act/); [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/04/29/ticket-act-passes-house-now-headed-to-senate/)
- Deceptive "official" sites and venue-name domains are targeted by the TICKET Act. — [Recording Academy](https://www.recordingacademy.com/advocacy/news/house-of-representatives-passes-ticket-act)
- The FTC alleges Ticketmaster let brokers exceed limits and profited from resale ("hundreds of millions"). This damages trust in the primary seller as well as in resale. — [Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/music/music-industry-news/ftc-sues-live-nation-illegal-resale-allegations-1236374721/); [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/ftc-sues-live-nation-ticketmaster-scalpers-1235430610/)
- Pro-resale view: an IPI study claims ticket resale saved fans $414M in 2024 (industry-aligned). — [IPI](https://www.ipi.org/ipi_issues/detail/study-ticket-resale-saved-fans-414-million-in-2024)

### Inferences
- A platform with its own identity-bound tickets (rotating barcodes) and in-app face-value transfer or resale removes both the fake-ticket and the speculative-listing problems. That's a trust claim Exos can make explicitly to fans.

### Gaps
- There's no US-wide 2023–2026 statistic on counterfeit or duplicate ticket incidence (FTC Consumer Sentinel ticket-fraud counts weren't found).
- Terms of the StubHub/Vivid Seats guarantees and fan satisfaction with them weren't researched in detail.
- Reddit sentiment wasn't collected.
